# Keyword mapping: Pamukkale Balon Turları

Each URL targets **one** primary search intent, and secondary terms support it without competing with another page.
Turkish (TR) is the default at `/`; English (EN) lives under `/en/`. No search-volume data was supplied, so check the priorities in Google Search Console after launch.

## Where each keyword is placed

Every page below has its primary keyword in **all 7 placements**: `<title>`, meta description, the single H1, the first paragraph, at least one H2, at least one image `alt`, and internal anchor text from other pages (footer link groups, related-article cards, breadcrumbs). This was checked by `kwaudit` on the built site (2 Nov 2026). The keyword is used once or twice per placement, with no stuffing.

Sources: titles/descriptions are in `src/lib/page-meta.ts` (pages) and the article bodies (`seoTitle`, `description`, `intro`) in `src/content/{tr,en}/part*.ts`. The article focus phrase is `kw` in `src/content/articles.ts`; it drives the summary H2 ('Özetle: …' / 'Key takeaways: …') and the footer 'Rehberler / Guides' links. Page H1/H2/alt text is in `src/components/pages/*.tsx`. Footer anchors are in `FOOTER_LINKS` in `src/components/layout.tsx`.

## Cannibalisation rules (follow these for future edits)

- `/fiyatlar/` owns the commercial "price" intent. The *prices* article owns the informational "what
  affects the price" intent. It should link to `/fiyatlar/` and must not compete for "fiyat" + "paket".
  The same split applies to `/en/prices/`.
- `/ucus-bilgileri/` (and `/en/flight-information/`) is the hub for timing, duration, clothing and
  safety. Today the FAQ links to 4 articles and the flight page links to 3. As content grows, add hub-to-article links there.
- The FAQ gives short answers and points to the matching article, so it does not target the article's long-tail query.
- Each article has a closing CTA to the reservation page in its own language.
- The "Main internal link target" column says where each article should send readers. Check the
  inline links in `src/content/*/part*.ts` when you edit an article (`src/test/content.test.ts` checks
  that every inline link resolves).
